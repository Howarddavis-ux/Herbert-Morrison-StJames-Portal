Our history, achievements, and the people who make us great.

School History

Herbert Morrison Technical High School was established in 1961 in the parish of St. James, Jamaica. Named in honour of Herbert Stanley Morrison, the school was founded with a mission to provide quality technical and academic education to the youth of western Jamaica.

Over the decades, the school has grown significantly in both student population and academic programmes. From its early focus on technical vocational training, it has expanded to offer a broad curriculum covering sciences, humanities, information technology, and the arts — all while maintaining its strong technical foundation.

Today, Herbert Morrison Technical High School stands as one of the leading secondary institutions in St. James, known for producing graduates who go on to distinguished careers in engineering, education, healthcare, business, and public service.

Landmark Accomplishments

  • Consistent CXC/CSEC pass rates above the national average for over 15 consecutive years.
  • Multiple Inter-Secondary School Boys and Girls Championships athletics medals.
  • Winners of the St. James Parish Science Fair (2018, 2020, 2023).
  • Recipients of the Ministry of Education School Improvement Grant (2019 & 2022).
  • Introduction of the Information Technology programme, now one of the most enrolled at the school.
  • Community outreach programmes recognised by the St. James Municipal Corporation.
