About Our School

Herbert Morrison Technical High School is a government-operated institution located in St. James, Jamaica. Founded in 1961, the school is committed to academic excellence, technical training, and the holistic development of its students. We serve the communities of St. James with pride, shaping future leaders and skilled professionals.
